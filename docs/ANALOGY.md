# What computational photolithography contributes

The useful mathematical pattern is inverse design through a medium: choose the input pattern by predicting its reproduced output, compare that output with an intended target, and optimize under input/manufacturing constraints. An audio master plays the role of the designed input, playback response the forward medium, a time-frequency target the desired output, and digital headroom an input constraint. Regularization stops unstable compensation of strongly attenuated frequencies; multiple model profiles support joint design across conditions.

The two-dimensional STFT makes local frequency-and-time targets possible. For example, the demo emphasizes bass only around known synthetic kick onsets. The dimensions are time and frequency, not the two spatial coordinates of a photomask. Merely plotting a spectrogram does not create a physical equivalence.

Optical imaging may include coherent/partially coherent fields, intensity formation, resist thresholds, chemical development, and binary or geometric mask constraints. Acoustic playback involves pressure waves, phase, room reverberation, direction, level-dependent transducers and limiters, and a listener's perception. Their nonlinearities, feasible sets and noise sources differ. Lithography objectives may be nonconvex, and this convex surrogate is not a theorem about lithography itself.

The diagonal STFT multiplication here is an explicitly defined virtual medium. Analysis-adjoint of the modified coefficients gives the rendered simulation WAV. In general, reanalyzing that waveform does not give the original modified coefficients: redundant STFT synthesis projects onto consistent images. Thus the reported coefficient-domain objective and simulated listening WAV are related but distinct; they are not measurements of a causal LTI speaker. Actual FIR convolution is not exactly diagonal in a windowed STFT, especially across transients. No room, firmware, SPL, auditory masking or commercial earbud data was measured.

Inverse-medium precompensation and regularized inverse filtering are established mathematical ideas. This prototype does not claim to invent those methods, or establish novelty merely by borrowing lithography terminology. Its concrete contribution here is a reproducible formulation, constrained solve, derivation and falsifiable synthetic experiment.
